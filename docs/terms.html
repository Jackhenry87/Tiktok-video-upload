Terms of Service — TikTok Videos (personal automation tool)

Last updated: July 2026

1. What this app is. TikTok Videos is a personal-use content tool operated by its owner. It creates original short-form videos and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

2. Single-user service. The app is not offered to the public. The only user is the account owner; no third-party accounts can connect to it.

3. Content. All published content is original, created by or for the owner. The app does not download, repost, or redistribute other creators' content.

4. Acceptable use. The app follows TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, enforces daily posting limits, and requires manual review of content before publishing.

5. Liability. The tool is provided as-is for personal use, with no warranties.

6. Contact. Questions: open an issue on this repository.
